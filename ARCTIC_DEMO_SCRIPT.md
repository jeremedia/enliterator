**Ask:** *"Compare Chinese and Russian Arctic strategies - where do they cooperate and compete?"*

**Expected Response Highlights:**

- Cooperation on Arctic shipping routes and resource development

### **Query 2: Climate-Security Nexus**
**Ask:** *"How does climate change affect Arctic security dynamics?"*

**Expected Response Highlights:**
- Melting ice opens new shipping routes
- Increased access to critical minerals and resources
- Environmental security vs traditional military security
- Impact on indigenous communities and sovereignty claims

**Ask:** *"What role do critical minerals play in Arctic competition?"*

**Expected Response Highlights:**
- Supply chain security and strategic autonomy
- Rare earth elements and technology dependencies
- Environmental protection vs resource extraction
- Economic development opportunities and challenges

### **Query 4: Nordic Cooperation Framework**
**Ask:** *"How do Nordic countries balance cooperation with great power rivalry in the Arctic?"*

**Expected Response Highlights:**
- Arctic Council as multilateral governance mechanism
- Innovation and technology leadership approach
- Balancing security concerns with environmental priorities
- Regional cooperation vs bilateral pressures

### **Query 5: Future Strategic Scenarios**
**Ask:** *"What are the key risks and opportunities for Arctic governance in the next decade?"*

**Expected Response Highlights:**
- Evolving threat landscape and military positioning
- Climate adaptation and environmental protection
- Indigenous rights and traditional knowledge integration
- Technology innovation and sustainable development
